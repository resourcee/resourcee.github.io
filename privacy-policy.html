Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 30, 2026. Resourcee LLC, a Wyoming limited liability company.

1. Who We Are

Resourcee LLC ("Resourcee," "we," "us," or "our") is a limited liability company registered in the State of Wyoming, USA. We operate a talent placement platform connecting US-based companies with South American engineering, design, and operations professionals. Our principal place of business is in the United States.

You may contact us at: privacy@resourcee.co

2. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, including:

  • Contact information: name, email address, phone number, company name, and job title when you fill out our contact form or schedule a call.
  • Business information: team size, hiring needs, and role requirements you share during our intake process.
  • Candidate information: if you are a job seeker, we may collect your resume, work history, skills, English proficiency scores, and references.
  • Usage data: IP address, browser type, pages visited, and time spent on our website, collected automatically via cookies and analytics tools.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Match client companies with qualified LATAM candidates.
  • Communicate with you about our services, your placements, and your account.
  • Send you relevant content and updates (with your consent).
  • Improve our website and services through analytics.
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce our agreements.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share your information with:

  • Client companies: we share candidate profiles (with candidate consent) with prospective employer clients.
  • Service providers: third parties that help us operate our business (e.g., email platforms, CRM tools, payment processors), bound by confidentiality agreements.
  • Legal requirements: when required by law, court order, or to protect our legal rights.

5. Data Retention

We retain personal data for as long as necessary to provide our services and comply with legal obligations. Client and candidate data is typically retained for 3 years after the end of the engagement, unless you request deletion earlier.

6. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:

  • Access, correct, or delete your personal data.
  • Withdraw consent at any time (where processing is based on consent).
  • Object to or restrict processing of your data.
  • Data portability.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at privacy@resourcee.co. We will respond within 30 days.

7. Cookies

Our website uses essential cookies to ensure basic functionality, and analytics cookies (e.g., Google Analytics) to understand how visitors use our site. You may disable analytics cookies through your browser settings. Essential cookies cannot be disabled without affecting website functionality.

8. Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, disclosure, or destruction. However, no internet transmission is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. International Transfers

As a US-registered company serving clients and candidates across the Americas, your data may be processed and stored in the United States and in the countries where our candidates are located. We take appropriate steps to ensure adequate protection of data transferred internationally.

10. Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed to individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes by posting the new policy on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued use of our services after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
