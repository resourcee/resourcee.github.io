Real Answers

Every CTO asks these six questions.

Here are straight answers, no spin, no sales pitch.

What about communication and language?

Every candidate passes a structured English fluency assessment, written and spoken. You interview them in English before any decision. Most clients say they can't tell the difference from a US hire.

Will they work in my timezone?

All LATAM engineers naturally overlap 6–8 hours with US business hours across EST, CST, and PST. No early-morning calls. No late-night standups. They're online when you are.

What if it doesn't work out after they start?

90-day free replacement, no questions asked. We've held a 98% match rate for years, but the guarantee means you carry zero risk. You don't pay us until you make the hire.

How is this different from typical offshore?

Nearshore, not offshore. LATAM engineers are timezone-aligned, culturally compatible with US teams, and we personally screen every candidate. You never get a CV dump or a body shop.

Who handles compliance, payroll, and contracts?

We handle everything, local employment contracts, benefits, tax compliance, and payroll in the engineer's country. You get one clean invoice. No legal complexity on your end.

What actually happens on the first call?

A 30-minute scoping call with a technical recruiter, not a salesperson. You'll leave with a hiring timeline, sample candidate profiles, and clear next steps. No pitch. No commitment.
