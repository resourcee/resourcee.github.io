Our Story

Built by engineers, for engineering teams

We got tired of watching great LATAM engineers get passed over, and watching US teams struggle to hire. So we built the bridge.

How We Started

We were the engineers who kept hiring wrong

Resourcee was founded by Rashad M. and Ala B., two engineers who had spent years building and scaling technical teams at US startups. They kept running into the same wall: hiring was too slow, too expensive, and too often produced the wrong result.

After working directly alongside engineers from Colombia, Argentina, Brazil, and Chile, they realized LATAM talent wasn't a workaround. It was often the best engineering talent they'd ever worked with. Sharp, driven, deeply collaborative, and in the same time zones.

The problem wasn't the talent. It was the access layer. Most US companies didn't know where to look, how to assess, or how to structure a nearshore team that actually integrates. Rashad and Ala did. So they built Resourcee to solve exactly that.

Today Resourcee has placed 500+ engineers, designers, and operations professionals into US teams as embedded nearshore partners, and we're just getting started.

What We Believe

Three things that drive everything we do

🎯

Quality over volume

We'd rather turn down a search than place the wrong person. Our vetting is rigorous because we know the cost of a bad hire, we've lived it. Every candidate we present is someone we'd hire ourselves.

🤝

Long-term partnership

We don't disappear after placement day. We check in at 30, 60, and 90 days. We want the engineer to thrive and the team to grow, that's when we know we've done our job right.

🌎

LATAM first

We're genuinely invested in building a bridge between exceptional South American talent and the world-class opportunities they deserve. Every placement we make moves that mission forward.
